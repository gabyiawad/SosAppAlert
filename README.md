# Privacy Policy for SOS Alert

**Effective Date: August 14, 2026**

Gia Software Solutions (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the **SOS Alert** mobile application (“App”).

## Information We Collect

### Phone Number

We collect your phone number to create, verify, and secure your SOS Alert account.

### Selected Contacts

SOS Alert may request permission to access your device contacts.

You choose which contacts to add as trusted contacts or alert recipients. We only use the contact information you select, such as the contact's name and phone number.

We do not use your contacts for advertising and do not sell or rent contact information.

### Location

With your permission, SOS Alert may access your location to:

* Include your location in an SOS alert.
* Share your current or live location with trusted contacts.
* Help alert recipients locate you.

You can manage location permission through your device settings.

### Alerts and Notifications

When you send or receive an alert, we may process information such as the sender, recipients, date and time, alert status, and location when enabled.

We may also use device notification tokens and related technical information to deliver push notifications and emergency alerts.

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

* Verify and maintain your account.
* Manage trusted contacts and groups.
* Send and receive SOS alerts.
* Share your location when requested.
* Deliver push notifications.
* Provide support and improve the reliability and security of the App.

We do **not** sell or rent your personal information.

## Sharing of Information

Information may be shared with the trusted contacts or group members you choose when you send an alert.

We may also use third-party service providers for services such as account authentication, cloud storage, notifications, phone verification, and subscription processing.

These providers process information only as necessary to provide their services.

## Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your information only as long as necessary to operate SOS Alert and provide its features.

You can update or remove trusted contacts at any time.

You may also delete your SOS Alert account from within the App where available, or contact us at **[support@giasoftwaresolutions.com](mailto:support@giasoftwaresolutions.com)** to request account or data deletion.

Some information already delivered to another user as part of an alert may remain in that user's alert history.

## Permissions

SOS Alert may request permission to access:

* **Contacts:** To let you select trusted contacts.
* **Location:** To share your location with SOS alerts.
* **Notifications:** To receive SOS alerts and important notifications.

You can manage these permissions through your device settings. Disabling certain permissions may limit some App features.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no electronic system can guarantee complete security.

## Emergency Services

SOS Alert helps users communicate with trusted contacts but is **not a replacement for police, ambulance, fire departments, or other official emergency services**.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted with an updated effective date.
